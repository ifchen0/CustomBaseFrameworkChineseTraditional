Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) translation for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813689040]Custom Base Framework[/url] by Evil Eye Guy.

為 Evil Eye Guy 的 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813689040]Custom Base Framework[/url] 提供繁體中文翻譯。

[h2]What is translated[/h2][list]
[*]Mod settings: faction switch, garrisons and performance, uninstall, error report, other mod options
[*]Quests, scenarios, settlement types, unit names, containers and dungeon level buttons
[*]The settlement editor
[/list]
Player-facing text is translated from English. Editor text is converted from the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3814954055]Simplified Chinese translation[/url] by KKRimWorld and adjusted to Taiwan terms. Terms follow the vanilla Traditional Chinese translation.

[h2]翻譯範圍[/h2][list]
[*]模組設定：派系開關、駐軍與效能、移除 CBF、錯誤回報、其他模組選項
[*]任務、腳本、據點類型、兵種名稱、容器與地牢樓層按鈕
[*]據點編輯器
[/list]
玩家會看到的文字以英文原文翻譯；編輯器文字由 KKRimWorld 的[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3814954055]簡體中文翻譯[/url]轉換為繁體，並調整為台灣用語。用詞依照原版遊戲的繁體中文翻譯。

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Load after Custom Base Framework.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]Translations for the Medieval Overhaul, VFE Medieval 2, VFE Classical, VFE Settlers, World Domination and A Rim of Ice and Fire integrations only load when those mods are active.
[*]Custom Base Framework updates often. Text added after this translation shows in English until the translation is updated.
[/list]
I made this because I wanted to play Custom Base Framework and no Traditional Chinese translation existed yet. If anyone would like to take over the translation, you are welcome to.
